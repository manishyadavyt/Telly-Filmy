Star Plus Ko Aakhir Kya Baat Khatak Rahi Hai? The Channel’s Cryptic Comments Leave the Internet Curious

Star Plus has sparked curiosity with its mysterious 'Baat Khatki' social media activity, leaving fans wondering if the cryptic campaign is connected to Anil Kapoor's upcoming reality show India Ke Top 1%.

By Telly Filmy
Aug 31, 2026 | 11:30 PM IST• 1 min read
Share:

Star Plus ke social media par aakhir chal kya raha hai? The channel has suddenly caught the ‘Baat Khatki’ fever and seems to be finding something unusual everywhere! From dropping ‘Baat Khatki’ in the comment sections of popular brands’ posts to changing its social media feed, Star Plus has turned its entire feed into one big mystery. Recently, popular Star Parivaar faces were also seen jumping on the bandwagon and hinting at #BaatKhatki.

Sources suggest that all this cryptic activity could be linked to Anil Kapoor’s exciting new reality show, India Ke Top 1%, premiering on 19 September. Is ‘Baat Khatki’ the first clue to what the show has in store?

Filhaal, the internet is confused, curious and waiting to find out ki Star Plus ko aakhir kaunsi baat khatak gayi!
